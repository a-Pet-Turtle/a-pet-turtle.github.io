Cyberdeck

Call it what you want. Technically, it is a cyberdeck, but it is still in a cardboard case. Here's what it's made of.

  • Raspberry Pi 3B+ (1 GB RAM)
  • Hosyond 7 inch capacitive touch DSI SPI display
  • 0.5 foot right-angle Micro USB to right-angle USB-A cable
  • Rii X1 Mini Keyboard
  • Miady HYD009 portable rechargable battery pack
  • Hot glue
  • Cardboard
  • String (yarn)
